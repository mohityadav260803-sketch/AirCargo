About SkyLane

We are an air cargo logistics company based at the cargo terminal of Ahmedabad airport. We handle the whole journey: pickup, screening, flight, customs and final delivery.

What we do
We book, consolidate, screen and fly cargo on scheduled and chartered flights, then clear customs and deliver to the consignee's door.
Who we serve
Exporters of pharmaceuticals, textiles and engineering parts, e-commerce sellers with urgent restocks, and manufacturers who cannot afford a stopped production line.
Where we operate
Our hub is Ahmedabad (AMD), with daily links to Delhi, Mumbai, Dubai, Singapore, Frankfurt and London, and pickup across Gujarat.
Why shippers choose us
One waybill number gives live status and a named duty manager for every shipment. Pharma-grade 2 to 8 °C handling is run in our own terminal, not outsourced.

Before you ship: rules and documents

  • ICEGATE (Indian Customs) File shipping bills and check export clearance status before your cargo reaches our terminal.
  • IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations Confirm packing and labelling rules before you book the DG add-on.
  • DGCA Check Indian civil aviation requirements that apply to carried cargo.
  • BCAS Understand the security screening rules that every consignment must pass.
